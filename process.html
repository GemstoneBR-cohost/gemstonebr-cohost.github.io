Our End-to-End Co-Hosting Process

Step-by-step professional management designed to transform your asset, optimize performance, and deliver maximum passive yields.

STEP 01

Initial On-Site Inspection & Audit

We perform a rigorous, physical inspection of the property to evaluate layout efficiency, physical condition, and structural readiness. Our operational experts identify every detailed feature that can be highlighted for guests.

  • Detailed baseline structure and utility health check
  • Count the existing furniture and daily necessities at the scene
  • Confirm the advantages and disadvantages of the property
STEP 02

Custom Property Enhancement Report

Based on the inspection, we generate a bespoke renovation and aesthetic proposal. We evaluate what is already in place to see what can be repurposed, and list precisely what additions are needed, optimizing investment cost.

  • Tailor an appropriate decoration plan based on the current situation
  • Detailed budget allocation and targeted upgrade list
  • Design plan optimized for high conversion booking rates
STEP 03

Standardized High-Value Facility Checklist

We develop a standardized inventory checklist based on years of OTA guest feedback. This list prioritizes high-impact amenities that directly boost guest satisfaction and booking rankings — ensuring your budget is spent only where guests see and feel it.

  • Prioritize the allocation of items that precisely meet the majority of customer needs
  • Prevents unnecessary design expenditures and saves owner capital
  • Can be used to evaluate the accommodation quality of hotels
STEP 04

Professional Media & Retouched Gallery

We employ professional wide-angle cameras, tailored lighting equipment, and specialized hospitality post-processing techniques to create a flawless online presence. These premium visuals serve as your listing's primary funnel magnet.

  • High-end DSLR wide-angle shooting and HDR exposure
  • Professional staging and aesthetic color correction
  • Meticulous post-editing tailored for global platform algorithms
STEP 05

Monthly Performance & Operational Reporting

Transparency is our signature. At the start of every month, we provide a thorough statement detailing occupancy rates, ADR, OTA fee breakdowns, and exact expenses. We fine-tune pricing dynamically to guarantee you maximum worry-free passive cashflow.

  • Transparent, itemized breakdowns of monthly revenue and costs
  • Dynamic pricing reviews customized for individual market trends
  • Worry-free passive returns: sit back and watch your yields grow
